Why this exists

Triad sells transparency and data-led pricing. The owner should be able to see both without calling anyone.

The site makes three promises: one simple fee, revenue over inflated occupancy, and pricing that is data-led with human review. Today the proof reaches owners through periodic reports and their account manager. When a month looks weak, the fastest way to learn why is a phone call. Owner Pulse makes the pricing work visible, so trust is built by the product instead of by support hours.

Hypotheses to test

  • Owners who see the reason behind a price drop are less likely to churn after a slow month.
  • A visible decision log cuts "why is my rate lower?" contacts to Owner Support.
  • Showing RevPAR next to the comp set ends the occupancy argument faster than any explanation on a call.

How I'd measure it

  • North star: 12-month owner retention.
  • Owner Support contacts per property per month, tagged by theme.
  • Weekly active owners in the portal.
  • Median time for an owner to approve a change that needs their OK.
  • Guardrail: analyst minutes per decision. The log can't become a writing job.

What I'd validate first

  1. Read the last 90 days of owner tickets and tag the top five themes.
  2. Shadow three account-manager performance reviews.
  3. Interview six owners: single property, small portfolio, and an owner who left.
  4. Check what the PMS and pricing tool already expose before building any data pipeline.

Risks and how the design handles them

  • Owners micromanage prices. They see suggestion, review and reason, but only approve changes that touch their terms, such as channel rates or minimum stays.
  • Analysts spend hours writing reasons. An AI draft from the model's own signals, which the analyst edits in one line.
  • Stale channel data erodes trust. Show data freshness on every number.

How this was built

Designed and coded in one session with Claude, starting from Triad's public site: the management-services page, the fee model, payout and tax rules, and the October 5 post on Vrbo's commission change. The visual language follows triadvacationrentals.com. Next step in a real cycle would be a Figma pass on the decision card with three owners before writing production code.
